!!! THE ICONICLES !!!

the iconicles is a telivision show from 2011 that aired on Cbeebies that i very much enjoyed.

it was about this guy called Nat who had created a device known as the iconi-screen which could transport the iconicles to him.

each episode would feature one - or two in the case of Splish and Splash - going to Nat's office. the episodes would go between this and an animated segment with one of the other iconicles in their environment.
Miss Moo lives Down On The Farm,
Rumble lives in the Wild Wilderness,
Splish and Splash live By The Water's Edge and
Skitter lives Around The Garden Hedge.
each of these locations also featured their own characters for them to interact with.
at the end of each episode Nat would ask the iconicle he was with some questions about what they saw on the iconi-screen

from what i can tell it aired from 2011 and contined to rerun episodes through to 2014
